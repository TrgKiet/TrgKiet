## Design Approach

I approach product design through a combination of:

**User Needs**  
Understanding what users are trying to achieve, what blocks them, and how the product can support their actual workflow.

**Business Goals**  
Designing solutions that are not only usable but also practical for the company, product team, and business model.

**System Thinking**  
Looking beyond individual screens to understand the full product structure, service flow, and operational process.

**Clear Interface Design**  
Creating interfaces that reduce confusion, support decision-making, and help users complete tasks efficiently.
