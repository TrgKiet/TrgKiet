# Brand Brief (Fill Me In)

About you
- Who are you in one sentence?
- What 3 adjectives should your brand feel like? (e.g., rigorous, playful, premium)
- What do you do best? (core strengths/services)
- What proof do you have? (outcomes, metrics, testimonials)

Audience
- Who are your ideal clients/employers? (titles, industries, geos)
- What do they value most when hiring you? (speed, craft, strategy, results)
- What objections do they have?

Positioning
- What differentiates you vs. peers?
- What work do you want more of? What work do you want less of?

Visual direction
- Light vs. dark preference?
- Color tolerance: neutral / restrained accents / bold
- Type vibe: classic serif / modern grotesk / experimental
- Reference 3 sites you like and 1 you dislike (and why)

Content
- Hero statement (try 8–12 words)
- Short bio (2–3 sentences)
- Project list (title, role, date, 1–2 line result)
- Contact details and social links

Constraints
- Timeline, must‑have pages, tech stack preferences

Success criteria
- After launch, what would make this a win? (e.g., X leads/month)
